## 🌍 WanderLux

Discover your next dream destination with WanderLux - a comprehensive travel booking platform that makes planning your getaway effortless.

### Features

- **Hotel Booking** - Browse and reserve accommodations at your destination
- **Transport Booking** - Book flights, buses, and other transportation options
- **Destinations Explorer** - Discover popular travel destinations and attractions
- **User Authentication** - Secure login and signup functionality
- **Admin Dashboard** - Manage bookings, users, and platform content

### Tech Stack

- **Backend** - PHP
- **Database** - MySQL
- **Frontend** - HTML, CSS, JavaScript

### Getting Started

Visit https://wanderlux.infinityfreeapp.com/ to explore destinations, book hotels and transport, and manage your travel plans.

### Usage

1. Sign up or log in to your account
2. Browse destinations and explore travel options
3. Book hotels and transport for your trip
4. Manage your bookings from your dashboard

_Admin users have access to additional platform management features._
